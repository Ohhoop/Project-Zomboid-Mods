title=Quick Restart
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/lja52Fs.gif[/img]
description=
description=Adds a Quick Restart panel on the death screen that gets you back into the game significantly faster than the normal process.
description=
description=Two options: Restart in a fresh world or respawn in the same one, both restoring your original character.
description=
description=Character is saved upon CREATION and reapplied automatically:
description=- Name, gender, profession
description=- Traits and skills
description=- Full appearance: hair, beard, hair color, skin tone, body hair, hair stubble, beard stubble
description=- All worn clothing with colors and texture variants
description=- Voice type, pitch, and prefix
description=- Spawn region
description=
description=The sandbox settings are captured automatically shortly after spawning. If they have changed since the world was created, the Fresh World button will prompt you to choose between the original creation settings and the current ones.
description=
description=If you choose to "Continue with a new character" instead of using Quick Restart, that new character will become the new original that gets reloaded on the next Quick Restart.
description=
description=If this mod is enabled mid-save, the Quick Restart buttons will be disabled since no character data was saved, as it is saved on character creation. You can "Continue with a new character" if you want to use the Fresh World button to specifically bring over your sandbox settings, for example.
description=
description=NEW FEATURE: NOW WITH CONFIGURABLE RANDOMIZERS
description=
description=A gear button on the Quick Restart panel opens the Restart Options window. Each category can be set to Keep (reapply your saved character) or Random:
description=- Gender: rolls a gender, with matching appearance, voice and first name
description=- Occupation: rolls an occupation; the traits it grants are guaranteed and your build is trimmed to stay within the point budget
description=- Traits: rolls a fresh set of traits that respects the world's trait points and mutual exclusions
description=- Clothing: dresses you in a random outfit from the game's outfit pool
description=- Spawn region: rolls a region among those available for the save, modded maps included
description=- Seed: generates a new world seed (Fresh World only)
description=- Zombies: fully randomizes the zombie settings (Fresh World only)
description=- Sandbox settings: fully randomizes everything else, zombies follow the Zombies option (Fresh World only). It is REALLY random and the world can turn out extreme or barely playable. You have been warned... twice, there is a warning popup too.
description=
description=Your choices are saved per save and carried over into the fresh world. Randomized results are always valid: trait points, exclusions and setting values are enforced, and on multiplayer servers the server validates them as well.
description=
description==========================================================================
description=
description=MOD COMPATIBILITY
description=
description=Quick Restart touches a lot of game mechanics. It reads and rewrites:
description=- Character creation: name, gender, occupation and traits
description=- Skills, XP and known recipes
description=- Appearance and voice
description=- Worn clothing and starter kit items
description=- Character and descriptor mod data (this is how modded character customization usually survives a restart)
description=- Spawn regions and map selection
description=- World creation: sandbox settings and world seed
description=- The death screen UI
description=
description=A mod that changes one of these mechanics in a way Quick Restart does not expect can cause compatibility issues. The most frequent one: the character or world data cannot be saved, and once you die the Quick Restart buttons tell you that no data was saved. If that happens to you, please post a message in the Bugs discussion with your mod list, I will see what I can do to widen the scenarios handled by the mod.
description=
description=Modded items that cannot be captured are simply skipped: they will not be reapplied on Quick Restart, everything else should be.
description=
description=Fresh world Quick Restart is single-player only. (You don't want to delete the world on which everyone else is playing!... Right?)
description=Same world Quick Restart in MP is available but still WIP, please let me know of any issues you encounter.
description=
description=Feel free to provide any feedback you may have in the comment section.
